# About CyberSec From Scratch

**CyberSec From Scratch** is an educational resource for anyone learning cybersecurity from the ground up. 

## Mission

> Understanding how attacks work is the first step to building better defenses.

This blog covers:
- **Red Team concepts** — explained from a defensive perspective
- **Blue Team techniques** — detection, prevention, and response
- **Lab exercises** — hands-on learning in safe, isolated environments
- **Career guidance** — certifications, skills, and pathways into cybersecurity

## Who Is This For?

- Students pursuing cybersecurity degrees
- IT professionals transitioning into security roles
- Self-taught learners building home labs
- Anyone curious about how digital attacks happen — and how to stop them

## Principles

1. **Education over exploitation.** Every post explains the defensive angle.
2. **Safety first.** Lab isolation and legal boundaries are non-negotiable.
3. **No complete attack tools.** We explain concepts, not distribute malware.
4. **Community-driven.** Corrections and contributions are welcome.

## Disclaimer

All content on this site is for **educational and authorized testing purposes only**. 
Never practice techniques from this blog on systems you do not own or have explicit written permission to test.

The author assumes no liability for misuse of this information.
